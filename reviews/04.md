### Non-blocking notes
- `shark_avoidance.h:18`: "others does not contain the shark itself" mostly repeats what the parameter name `others` says. I kept it as a contract: if it is broken, every frame silently takes over. It is a judgement call.
- `avoidSharks` picks a side first (tie rule) and then prefers straight only if straight scores strictly higher than that side. If the tie rule picked the slightly lower side, straight is compared against that side. The spec does not say how straight ties are handled, so this reading is acceptable.
- The tie test is named "with left ahead by less than the tie", but on its own it cannot show that left scores higher. The reviewer test with a 0.15 m offset confirms the offset favours left. Together they cover the claim.
- `otherSharks` copies every body line for every shark, every frame. That is negligible at 10 sharks. Noted only because the spec rules out performance work.
