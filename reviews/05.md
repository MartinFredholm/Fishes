### Non-blocking notes
- The hard step now does about half the steering at 10 sharks. Shark avoidance changes the commands in 43–55 % of shark frames (seeds 1–4). Mean shark speed drops to about 0.85 m/s, with v_cruise at 1.0. At 2 sharks it is 1–11 % and about 1.13 m/s. The Goal says "a retuned soft separation does most of the work, and a hard shark avoidance step catches what it misses". This tuning meets R21 the other way round. That is not a criterion, but you may notice it in the R12 visual check as sharks often swimming slowly at full turn.
- `SHARK_AVOID_HORIZON` = 8 s is about one full turn circle at cruise (2π·1.25 m / 1 m/s ≈ 7.9 s). So a commanded full turn is checked over its whole circle, and the speed cap at v_min sets in early. This is a likely cause of the high takeover share. I did not verify that.
- The two edited tests keep their intent. "Outside contact distance plus margin" now derives its offset from the constants, so it follows any retune. "The trigger uses the commanded path" moved the other shark to the top of the left turn circle, (10, 5.5). Its new `REQUIRE` guards the straight case. The reviewer test "side by side inside contact distance plus margin" still uses a fixed 0.5 m. That is still between 0.36 and 1.96 m, so it holds, but it would go stale if the margin dropped below 0.14 m.
