## Contact probe
- Location: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-martinfredholm-Documents-Fishes/63c3c2fa-a2f8-4471-b2a9-b10f97d775b8/scratchpad/probe/`. This is a session scratchpad. It may be cleared, so the procedure below is the durable record.
- `make_probe.py` copies a source tree, optionally overrides `constexpr float` constants (`NAME=VALUE`), and turns `fishes.cpp` headless. It sets `N_SHARKS`, seeds with `SetRandomSeed(seed)` in place of `InitWindow`, uses a fixed dt of 1/60 s for 7200 frames, and replaces drawing with the counter in `probe_tail.cpp`. The counter follows the spec's procedure: 11 samples per segment, a 0.36 m threshold, onsets counted after 5 s.
- `run_probe.sh <src dir> <n sharks> <label> [NAME=VALUE …]` builds with the real build's flags: libraries with `-O3`, and `fishes.cpp` with `-O3 -ffast-math -mcpu=native` plus OpenMP. It runs seeds 1–4 (or `PROBE_SEEDS`) and prints the per-seed and mean rates. With `PROBE_DIAG=1` it also logs every contact onset (angle, wall distance, speed, time since avoidance or the wall safety filter last acted) and the takeover share.
- The baseline was measured on a `git archive` export of `1ba5937`.

## Deviations
- Two tests from issue 04 were changed because their fixtures assumed the 0.2 m margin. What they check is unchanged:
  - "side by side … outside contact distance plus margin: no takeover": the offset is now `SHARK_CONTACT_DISTANCE + SHARK_AVOID_MARGIN + 0.04` (it was 0.6 m, which was 0.04 m beyond the old threshold).
  - The reviewer's "the trigger uses the commanded path, not the current heading": the held-still body line moved from (11.25, 6.75) to the top of the left-turn circle (10, 5.5). The straight path now clears it by more than contact distance plus margin, and a `REQUIRE` states that premise. The left turn still runs into it.
  - Both still fail under mutation: raising the trigger threshold by 0.1 m fails the first, and checking the trigger on a straight path or halving the margin fails the second.

## Not verified
- The rates are very sensitive to floating-point details, because the simulation is chaotic. Building the probe with `-O2` instead of `-O3` changed the start-value rate from 2.74 to 2.35. Poisson noise on the 4-seed mean is about ±0.4/min at the final values.
- The probe uses a fixed dt of 1/60 s. The real program uses the frame time, capped at 1/30 s, so on-screen runs differ from probe runs.
- Cost per frame of the 8 s horizon (240 steps × 4 candidates × (N − 1) others) was not profiled. Both the probe and the windowed run at 10 sharks ran smoothly by eye.
- About half the remaining 10-shark contacts at the start values were head-on wall traps (both noses within about 1.4 m of a wall). The spec accepts these. I did not repeat that breakdown at the final values: the four contacts seen were away from walls (1.4–4.4 m), with both sharks at `v_min` and in takeover.
